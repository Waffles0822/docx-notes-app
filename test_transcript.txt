Good morning everyone. Today we will discuss the concept of machine learning.
Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that enables systems to learn from data.
The three main types are supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning.
Supervised learning uses labeled data to train models. For example, classifying emails as spam or not spam.
Unsupervised learning finds patterns in unlabeled data. For example, grouping customers by purchasing behavior.
Reinforcement learning uses rewards and punishments to train agents. For example, training a robot to walk.
The key equation for linear regression is y equals mx plus b where m is the slope and b is the intercept.
